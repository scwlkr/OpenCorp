# OpenCorp

Language for the persistent, autonomous software factory company.

## Language

**Software factory**:
A persistent company that selects useful problems, produces usable software and maintains its products within Owner authority and available resources.

**Product**:
Software for a specific audience that solves a concrete problem. A repository, scaffold or report alone is not a product.

**Product delivery**:
An accessible deployment or installable release with basic instructions and a working core workflow for its intended users, inside or outside the company.

**Product adoption**:
People or agents using a product to accomplish useful work. Activity that serves no actual need does not establish adoption.

**Product portfolio**:
The products the company develops and maintains. Leadership balances expansion against delivery and upkeep capacity.

**Product lifecycle**:
Idea generation, research, prototyping, building, marketing, sales, support and long-term maintenance. Delivery begins a product's useful life rather than ending company responsibility.

**Project**:
A finite effort toward a useful outcome for a product or the company. Products and their responsibilities can continue after a project finishes.

**Employee**:
A persistent company member with identity, specialty, knowledge and performance history. Employees persist while idle and when their cognitive engine changes.

**Employee skill**:
Maintained Markdown describing an employee's specialized competence and working methods. Management and collaborators improve it rapidly from experience; skills do not grant authority.

**Employee run**:
An execution of an employee's assignment using a particular model and supplied context. A new run or model does not create a new employee.

**Run inspection**:
Examining an employee run's supplied context, actions, outputs and operating conditions to understand progress or diagnose a problem. Recorded events, employee explanations and inferred diagnoses remain distinguishable.

**Department**:
A persistent specialized institution with leadership, employees, shared knowledge and standing responsibility. Departments may originate useful work between projects.

**Factory improvement**:
Internal work that removes a concrete delivery bottleneck. Useful internal products can receive an early preference and are judged by adoption.

**Blocked work**:
An unfinished commitment whose current approach cannot advance. Management seeks other authorized ways to achieve the same outcome before setting it aside.
_Avoid_: Resolved failure when only its cause has been recorded

**Owner escalation**:
A decision reserved for the Owner or indispensable input the company cannot obtain through permitted alternatives. It includes management's recommendation and the exact input needed.

**Product outcome**:
A useful result delivered toward the mission. Tests, reviews and operational records support judgment; producing them alone is not the outcome.

**Low-power mode**:
Company operation with minimal local resource use, retaining suitable lightweight or hosted work. It reduces local load without requiring the entire company to stop.

**Spending authorization**:
An explicit Owner-approved total ceiling for named work, permitted providers/models and an expiration. Unused allowance is not general spending permission, and estimates are not guarantees.

# Executive email

One shared company address is sufficient. Employees retain their identities and signatures in the shared company conversation; they do not require separate paid mailboxes. The CEO owns the daily report and consolidates relevant executive contributions. `skills/executive-email.md` supplies report guidance at execution time; `send_message` with `recipientId:"owner", channel:"email"` sends separate substantive reports/proposals after the employee run succeeds.

## Transport and setup

The daemon uses a small Cloudflare Email Routing Worker, the existing verified Owner destination and a temporary Workers KV inbox. It never needs access to the Owner's personal Gmail. Cloudflare's [pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/platform/pricing/) makes sending to verified destinations free; use existing Free-plan allowances for Worker/KV execution. Do not enable paid sending, change subscriptions or remove the binding's destination restriction. Recheck current terms and allowance before deployment.

1. Use an existing Cloudflare-managed domain with Email Routing enabled. Verify the Owner destination address in Cloudflare. Preserve existing domain routes.
2. Deploy `src/server/email-worker.ts` using the repository's installed dependencies and Wrangler, with `nodejs_compat`. Create a dedicated KV namespace bound as `INBOX`. Configure `send_email` as `[{"name":"EMAIL","destination_address":"owner@example.net"}]`, and Worker variables `MAILBOX:"ceo@example.com"`, `OWNER:"owner@example.net"`. The mailbox local part must be at most seven characters so signed reply addresses fit SMTP limits. Disable Worker observability/log capture for this private transport.
3. Generate independent random 32-byte hex `TOKEN` and `REPLY_KEY`, store them as Worker secrets, and retain matching values in private service configuration. No account-wide Cloudflare token enters the daemon or employee context.
4. Route only the company address to this Worker and enable Email Routing subaddress matching, so replies to `ceo+...@example.com` reach it. The signed suffix verifies possession of the report delivered to the configured Owner. An unsigned address, forged suffix or different sender is rejected. Forwarding a report also forwards its reply capability; keep it private. Ordinary email text grants no spending or access; [Owner proposals](OWNER_PROPOSALS.md) bind explicit decisions to their original message.
5. Create `<data-root>/credentials/email.json`, mode `0600`, with `mailbox`, `owner`, HTTPS `endpoint`, `token`, `replyKey`, and `dailyHourUtc` (0–23). Keep this outside Git. Reinstall the supported service after a consistent backup. Configuration is opt-in; absent settings leave email disabled. Identity/key changes require reconciliation of retained integration and pending effects.

The daemon checks relay identity, then queues one daily confidential CEO assignment at/after the configured UTC hour. It does not accumulate missed days while an earlier report remains unfinished. A stopped company sends nothing; the relay may retain incoming replies. Local routing and current approved employee skills apply to report and reply work. Incoming replies identify the original shared message and return to its active author, otherwise the CEO. Executives use existing shared messages and authorized work rather than asking the Owner to forward mail.

## Recovery and practical limits

SQLite retains outgoing intent before dispatch, confirmed receipts and incoming text before relay acknowledgment. Interruption leaves an uncertain send held. `opencorp email-reconcile <actionId> delivered|absent <evidence>` records Owner inspection of delivery records and the receiving mailbox. Only one retry is permitted after confirmed absence. Do not create a replacement message to evade a held send. Backups preserve newer intake, pending daily work and completed undispatched replies; restore cannot erase observed effects.

Cloudflare KV temporarily retains accepted replies for 30 days, deleting them after durable local intake. Its eventual consistency can replay an acknowledged message; SQLite deduplication prevents duplicate work. Keep the company online within that retention period. Replies must contain plain text, at most 20,000 characters; attachments and large messages are rejected. No private reply address or credential is supplied to employees. The relay always sends to its configured verified Owner destination, regardless of caller content.

A successful provider receipt establishes acceptance, not human reading or employee report accuracy. Verify one real report, a contextual Owner reply and service interruption/restart before claiming live operation. Focused automated checks cover authentication, confidential routing and recovery; they are not substitutes for that exchange.

# Exact Owner proposals

Use the existing corporate command `owner.propose` with `title`, finished Markdown-guided `content`, `proposalScope`, future ISO `expiresAt`, and `channel` (`telegram` or `email`). Optional `actionId` reserves one existing undispatched product action with known cost/evidence and current policy. The command returns the immutable proposal in `attention` and queues its message through the existing transport after the employee run succeeds. Authenticated local Owner commands can also queue a proposal without inventing an employee run.

The service appends the exact scope, expiration, any linked action snapshot and reply instructions. In Telegram, use Reply on the original proposal after all parts arrive. In email, reply to that proposal's signed reply address. Send exactly `APPROVE <full-proposal-id>` or `DENY <full-proposal-id>`. A standard English `On … wrote:` tail containing only quoted or blank lines is allowed; remove signatures, conditions and other text from the authored reply. Bare yes/no, quoted approvals, wrong proposal IDs and replies to unrelated messages cannot authorize anything. An unresolved response needs a new unambiguous reply; it never becomes a grant later automatically.

Sender identity and reply linkage use the existing numeric private Telegram identity or signed email reply capability plus configured Owner sender. Keep signed addresses private: forwarding a proposal forwards its email reply capability. Credentials stay outside employee context. Proposals and Owner replies are excluded from hosted employee reads.

The first valid disposition is retained atomically with any action approval. Later replies cannot reverse or replay it. Silence stays pending. Expired proposals or changed policy/action scope cannot approve a grant. Restart and restore retain decisions and receipts; restore still quarantines effects and changes policy revision. A reserved product action also checks exact frozen scope and expiry at dispatch and keeps existing run, review and policy checks. A successor run may continue only the same employee and original assignment after the original run has ended and been revoked; its dispatch run is recorded without rewriting the frozen origin. Existing GitHub delivery adoption preserves this scope and refreshes its separate cost preflight. Management uses existing assignment controls to resume the original work. Approval prepares that one action; it does not execute it or promise successful delivery. Denial leaves it blocked. Failed, uncertain or dispatched actions cannot be repackaged into a fresh proposal to evade reconciliation.

A proposal without `actionId` records only the Owner's decision about its stated scope. No generic command, access change, legal commitment or spending policy is automatically applied. Existing specialized controls remain necessary to execute such changes. Paid-inference budgets are separate work; this command cannot expand the global $0 allowance or confer unused general authority. Already-linked actions cannot be re-proposed or approved through the older standalone cost control; retain the denied/expired record and have management choose a permitted next step.

Composition and occasional reminders live in `skills/management.md`, `skills/owner-conversation.md` and `skills/executive-email.md`. Remind against the original proposal instead of creating duplicate grants. Inspect its `attention.disposition` and incoming `message.proposalResponse`; recorded outcomes are authoritative, employee explanations are commentary. Narrow tests exercise sender, scope, expiration and replay boundaries with nonbillable local actions. Actual replies through both configured channels remain required to qualify a deployment.
